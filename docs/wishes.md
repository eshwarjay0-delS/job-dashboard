*The owner's running list. Every directive, idea, and wish from the owner lands here
with a status. The Plan & Vision hub reads this file live — update it the moment a
status changes. Format: `- [status] Title (date) — note`. One line per wish.*

`building` (in progress now) · `done` (shipped, verified) · `rejected` (owner said no
or superseded) · `blocked` (waiting on the owner or an external step).*
