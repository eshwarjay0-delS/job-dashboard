## Prior decision (20 November 2026)

- Explicit candidate-confirmed skills may be represented naturally in relevant existing project bullets across selected resume roles, including earlier roles, without requiring the source bullet to name the tool.
- Confirmation supports the skill only; it does not authorize new employers, projects, titles, dates, locations, metrics, outcomes, contacts, credentials or unrelated actions.
- Team, colleague, client, organization and other third-party attribution is not candidate confirmation.
