# Poke: live REA, 23 October 2026

Method: **REA tool attempt + public reference review.** The home page and 25 first-party scripts were saved, the
Reverse Engineering plugin mapped the network surface, and the visible text of 9 public pages was saved (home, pricing,
FAQ, recipes, the Gmail recipe, get-started, docs, creating recipes, explore). Nothing was run, nothing is copied into
MFit. Observed on 23 Oct unless marked *inferred*. The earlier `poke-gmail` entry shaped MFit's Gmail and WhatsApp
updates; this entry is the live product today.

## How it is built (observed)

- Next.js; **no public source maps.** Paths seen: `/api/v1/user/profile`, `/api/v1/subscription/status`,
  `/api/v1/auth/logout`, `/login`. Installable web app (`manifest.webmanifest`).
- Seen in the scripts: Supabase, PostHog, Sentry; a trust-centre widget (`cdn.delve.co`); files on Cloudflare R2.
- Made by Interaction (poke@interaction.co). Home page banner: **"Poke is joining Cognition"**. This confirms the
  acquisition that only one outside source reported.

## What the user sees (observed)

- **Promise:** "your new favorite contact". Proactive, private, personal, inside your texts: **Messages, WhatsApp,
  Telegram**, by text or voice message.
- **Integrations:** Gmail and Outlook (mail, calendar, contacts), Notion, GitHub, Linear, Oura and more; anyone can
  add custom integrations through **MCP**.
- **Recipes:** a recipe bundles the context Poke needs from the user, a prefilled first message and the required
  integrations, with a **share or install link**. Built in "Kitchen" or with a command-line tool; edits to a
  published recipe only affect new sign-ups.
- **Proactive:** uses your connected apps and its memory to act on time (example shown: action items from Granola
  added to the calendar). Reminders and head starts on tasks from a text.
- **Extras:** builds and hosts a personal website at a handle; **Poke Human** sends real-world tasks (bookings, calls,
  orders) to a person, who sees only what the task needs.
- **Account:** delete from settings (up to 24 hours), choose which channel it texts you on.

## Ideas worth taking for MFit

1. **The assistant lives where people already text** (observed). MFit's WhatsApp bot is this; Telegram and iMessage
   would widen it (*inferred*).
2. **Recipes with an install link** (observed). *Inferred:* an MFit recipe could be "Watch my inbox for interview
   invites and prep me", or a staffing firm sharing one setup with every candidate.
3. **Every plan gets the whole product; plans add intelligence and limits** (observed).
4. **Upgrade by asking in the chat** (observed).
5. **Proactive actions from connected apps** (observed): MFit's watcher already does this for mail; calendar and
   interview prep are the next step.
6. **A human for the real-world part, who sees only what the task needs** (observed). *Inferred:* for MFit, a human
   recruiter step for rate calls or RTR confirmations, with the same data limits.
7. **Delete the account from settings, with the timeline stated** (observed).
8. **MCP for custom integrations** (observed). This matches the open MCP item from the Tsenta study.
