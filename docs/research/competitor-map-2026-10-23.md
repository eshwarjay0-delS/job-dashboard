# Competitor map, 23 October 2026: who to REA, and the cherries worth taking

Owner request (23 Oct): "deduct more and more competitors as well. we'll REA all of them and take as many sweet
cherries and deal that are already user appealed from everything from UI to functionality to marketing to pricing
models."

**Method: public reference review (web search, 23 Oct).** Nothing here was observed in the products themselves:
this environment could not reach their sites (network policy; the allowlist the owner added applies to a new session).
Prices come from third-party roundups, many written by competitors, and they disagree; each is "reported", not
verified. The "cherry" lines are our reading of what users are shown to value. They are hypotheses for the live REA
to confirm, not findings. We document behavior and public interfaces for our own design; we never copy their code,
text or assets.

## 1. Live interview copilots (Kompas competes here)

| Product | Reported pricing model | Reported price points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ParakeetAI | Pay per credit (about one hour each), plus an unlimited plan | $38/credit; 15 for $177; unlimited $149.90/mo |
| Final Round AI | Subscription, monthly/quarterly/yearly; no free live sessions | $150/mo; $25/mo billed yearly |
| LockedIn AI | Subscription with credits; free basic tier; every platform incl. iOS/Android | $29.99–$109.98/mo (sources conflict) |
| Verve AI | Free tier (3 × 15-min sessions/mo) | Standard $44.99/mo, $16.99/mo yearly |
| Sensei AI | Free 15-min sessions | $89/mo, $24/mo yearly |
| CoPilot Interview | Free 15 min every day, no card; hour packs | $14.99–$19.99/mo; packs from $5.99 |
| Interview Sidekick | Flat pricing, free tier | Ultimate $160/mo |
| LastRound AI, InterviewFox, Ophy AI | Comparison-blog vendors | not collected |

Cherries to test: a **free daily allowance** (15 minutes a day, no card) as the trial; **hour packs** next to a
subscription for people with one interview; **every platform** (LockedIn's phone apps); the "unlimited" anchor plan
that makes a per-hour price look small.
