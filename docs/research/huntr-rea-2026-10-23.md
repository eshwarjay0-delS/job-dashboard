# Huntr: live REA, 23 October 2026

Method: **REA tool attempt + public reference review.** Public pages of huntr.co were fetched (home, /pricing,
/product/job-tracker, /product/resume-tailor, /product/job-application-autofill, /product/job-search-metrics,
/product/contact-tracker, /product/interview-tracker, /product/job-clipper, /product/targeted-resume,
/product/ai-resume-builder, /mobile, /product-updates) with their 2 first-party scripts. Nothing downloaded was run.
The Reverse Engineering plugin's `extract_endpoints.py` mapped the network surface. Captures stay in the session
scratchpad; no Huntr code, text or images are copied into MFit. Everything below is what their own public pages
showed on 23 Oct, unless marked *inferred*.

**Blocked:** nothing on huntr.co was refused. Third-party scripts (jQuery, Wistia, GA, DataFast, Rewardful) were
not fetched, by design. The app behind /login and /signup was not opened, because it needs an account.

## How it is built (observed)

- The marketing site is plain server-rendered HTML with jQuery and Bootstrap-style classes, not a JS framework.
  **No source maps** (only two small first-party scripts: a cookie helper and Cloudflare's email-hiding script).
- Behind Cloudflare: email obfuscation (`/cdn-cgi/…`) and Cloudflare Images (`imagedelivery.net`) for every picture.
- Outside services: Google Analytics 4, DataFast analytics (`datafa.st`), Rewardful affiliate tracking
  (`r.wdfl.co`), Wistia video.
- No API paths in the public pages. The app lives at `/home` after sign-in. The product-updates page documents an
  organisation API (members, invitations) and **webhooks with filters** for their advisor platform.
- Surfaces: web app, Chrome extension (Web Store id `mihdfbecejheednfigjpdacgeilhlmnf`), iOS and Android apps.

## What the user sees (observed)

- **Main promise:** one place for the whole job search: tracker, tailored resumes, cover letters and one-click
  application autofill ("like having 9 apps in one"). Social proof: "500,000+ job seekers", extension rated 4.9 from
  about 1.1k reviews.
- **Onboarding:** "Sign up for free", no trial of Pro; the free plan includes a few AI credits to test with.
- **Tracker:** Kanban board with drag-and-drop stages; an activity timeline per job from applied to offer; private
  notes; documents stored per job (the tailored resume and cover letter for that role); **Map View** of job
  locations to judge the commute; company data pre-filled from a company database.
- **Job Clipper (extension):** saves title, company, location, link and full description in two clicks, so the
  posting survives being taken down. Highlights keywords on the posting to add to your resume.
- **Autofill:** fills application forms on "1000s of sites" and saves the job to the board as you apply.
- **Contacts:** name, title, company, email, phone, LinkedIn and GitHub handles, linked to the job. Pitched as
  keeping recruiters off your personal phone.
- **Metrics:** funnel from applied to interview to offer, application cadence per week, a chart of application and
  interview dates.
- **Resume Tailor:** matches on four dimensions (keywords, responsibilities, qualifications, and more), not keyword
  overlap alone. Each suggestion names the job requirement it covers and gives a short **"Why"**. Match score shows
  before and after. Bulk accept or reject. Tone calibrated to junior, mid or executive level.
- **Resume builder:** 7 templates, 13 ATS-safe open fonts with embedded character maps, custom sections that import
  detects automatically, PDF export, and (since Dec 2025) a **content-only DOCX export** with plain styling.
- **Organisations ("Huntr Advisor"):** for bootcamps, career centres, coaches and outplacement. Groups of job
  seekers, CSV invites, required profile fields with completion status, private notes on each job seeker, a timeline
  of each seeker's activity, custom permission groups, sending job posts to chosen groups.
- **Their numbers:** "Tailored resumes get 2× more interviews", from their own 2025 report (1.7M applications,
  225K+ resumes, a 1,000-person survey). That is observational data from their own users, so it shows a link, not
  that tailoring causes the difference (*inferred*).

## Ideas worth taking for MFit

1. **Every tailoring suggestion says which job requirement it covers and why** (observed). It makes the change
   checkable by the candidate and the recruiter.
2. **Match on responsibilities and qualifications, not only keywords** (observed).
3. **The advisor model fits a staffing firm** (observed): groups of candidates, CSV import, required profile fields
   with a completion check, private recruiter notes, a per-candidate activity timeline, permission groups. This is
   close to the multi-candidate dashboard `CLAUDE.md` asks for (*inferred*).
4. **Save the full job description when a job is added** (observed). Postings disappear before interviews.
5. **A funnel per candidate:** applied, interview, offer, plus weekly cadence (observed). Fits per-candidate stats.
6. **Free tier capped by count (100 jobs), not by time** (observed). Clear, and it lets a user judge the product.
7. **A content-only DOCX export** with plain styling, next to the designed PDF (observed). Matches MFit's ATS-clean
   .docx goal.
8. **Contacts linked to the job** (observed): recruiter, hiring manager, and what was last discussed.
9. **Map View for commute** (observed). Lower priority for remote and C2C roles (*inferred*).
10. **Webhooks with filters for organisations** (observed). A later option if partners want MFit events.

**Not recommended to copy:** their sample "after" bullets add precise figures (transaction volumes, percentages)
that the "before" bullet never contained. For a staffing firm that is a fabrication risk. **"Apply to hundreds of jobs
in a single day"** is mass-apply framing that clashes with tailored, per-candidate submissions (*inferred*).
