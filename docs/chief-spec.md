# MarketFit Chief: the owner's spec (public summary)

The owner's spec of 23 Oct 2026 is the plan of record for the Chief. The full text is in the private parfAct
repository (`docs/chief/2026-10-23-owner-chief-spec.md`) because it names vendors, recruiters and a client.

**What it is:** one agent in WhatsApp that runs the job hunt while he is away. He briefs it in plain words; it works
overnight; the finished work is on his phone by morning. Nothing sends, files or posts without his yes.

**The rule:** public data only; a never-contact list of vendors he names; never auto-send; never invent what is not on
the resume; report in plain words.

**Overnight run:** from one brief ("watch my submissions tonight") the Chief makes its own jobs: scan inboxes, triage
(interview invites, RTRs, rate confirmations, document requests), double-submit check, follow-up drafts for threads
quiet 7+ days, refresh Apply matches. A 7am report lists what needs his call.

**Goal decomposition:** one goal (get submitted for a req, prep for a debrief, find N postings this week) becomes
scheduled sub-jobs he never writes.

**Blank for the owner:** resume gaps named, never filled; rates never guessed; submissions never assumed filed;
employer claims from recruiters never echoed, only flagged.

**Funnel in every report:** inbox (new → worth it → need calls → 0 sent without him), submissions (chains → keeper →
stand-downs), Apply (new → above 90% → saved).

**Approval cards:** every draft with Send, Edit, Skip.

**Not built, by his decision:** Slack or Teams, per-seat pricing, broad integrations (only Gmail, WhatsApp, the resume
and the pipeline), anything that moves without his tap.

**The line:** "I give the goal, the Chief does the steps, I only make the calls — and nothing moves without my yes."
