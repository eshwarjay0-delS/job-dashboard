## 4. Probe questions

A big true claim still draws a scope test. A claim this resume cannot support has to be declined.

- Probe: "What did you break, and how did you get back?"
- Follow-up: "Walk me through the exploit path you would use on that Gateway Load Balancer design."

- Probe: "Tell me about a finding you got into the report."
- Follow-up: "What was the severity, and who owned the fix?"
- Follow-up: "Was that an assumed-breach job or a black box?"
- Safe shape: there is no engagement on the resume. No severity, no report, no customer. He restates the build work, then the ramp, in future tense.
